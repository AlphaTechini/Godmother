# canvas

The studio's right-hand canvas: brief, photos, looks, lookbook and shopping.

**Decisions and tradeoffs:** The canvas shows current state and the chat shows history, so results stay visible as the conversation moves on.

## Files

- [BriefView.svelte](BriefView.svelte): The event brief on the canvas.
- [Canvas.svelte](Canvas.svelte): The right-hand canvas and its sections.
- [LookbookView.svelte](LookbookView.svelte): The finished look: full, face and hand panels, plus the video button.
- [LookCard.svelte](LookCard.svelte): One of the three looks, with its pieces and render.
- [LooksView.svelte](LooksView.svelte): The three looks side by side.
- [PhotoSlot.svelte](PhotoSlot.svelte): One photo upload slot.
- [PhotosView.svelte](PhotosView.svelte): The three photo slots and the consent notice.
- [RenderSteps.svelte](RenderSteps.svelte): Progress for each render step.
- [SectionHead.svelte](SectionHead.svelte): A canvas section heading.
- [ShoppingView.svelte](ShoppingView.svelte): The priced shopping list.
